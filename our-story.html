How We Started

Built on a simple idea

Morocco Boutique Tours was founded on the belief that the best way to experience Morocco is slowly, personally, and through the people who call it home — not from behind the window of a large coach shared with strangers.

We work with a small circle of trusted local guides and independent riads, and every journey — Signature or Tailor-Made — comes with your own private driver-guide and vehicle from the moment you land.
