Camel Trekking in Merzouga: What It's Really Like

Camel trekking across the Erg Chebbi dunes is the single most anticipated moment on most Sahara itineraries — and it tends to be gentler, and more atmospheric, than most first-timers expect.

Experiences · 6 min read · Morocco Boutique Tours

How It Works

Despite the common phrase, you'll actually be riding a dromedary — a one-humped camel — saddled and led in a caravan line by an experienced local guide on foot, rather than ridden independently. Camels kneel to let you mount, which involves a slightly jarring forward-then-back lurch as they stand, but the walking pace itself is slow, rhythmic, and surprisingly smooth.

The Sunset Trek to Camp

Most itineraries time the trek for late afternoon, setting off from the edge of Erg Chebbi as the light softens and the dunes shift from gold to deep orange and pink. The pace is unhurried by design — this isn't a race across the sand, but a slow, quiet approach into camp, often the first real moment of stillness after a day of driving.

How Long Does It Take?

The trek to camp typically takes 45 minutes to an hour, depending on which camp you're staying at and how deep into the dunes it sits. It's comfortable for most fitness levels and ages, though very young children or travelers with certain mobility or back conditions may prefer the 4x4 alternative some camps offer.

Photography Tips

The low, golden sunset light is genuinely spectacular on the dunes, and most guides are happy to pause briefly for photos along the way. A phone or lightweight camera is easier to manage on a moving camel than heavier gear; a wrist strap or crossbody bag helps keep it secure.

Sandboarding & 4x4 Alternatives

On longer itineraries with a full desert excursion day, sandboarding down the Erg Chebbi's larger dunes is a popular add-on, and a 4x4 vehicle covers ground camels can't reach as efficiently — useful for visiting more remote nomad encampments as part of a fuller day in the dunes.

Frequently Asked

Camel Trekking in Merzouga: Quick Answers

Is camel trekking uncomfortable?

Most travelers find it comfortable, if a little unfamiliar at first — the walking pace is slow and steady, though mounting and dismounting involves a brief forward-and-back motion as the camel kneels and stands.

How long is the camel ride to a Sahara desert camp?

Typically 45 minutes to an hour, depending on the specific camp's location within the Erg Chebbi dunes.

Can children ride camels in Merzouga?

Yes, camel treks generally suit most ages, though very young children may be more comfortable with a 4x4 transfer instead — ask us when planning and we can accommodate this.

What should I wear for camel trekking?

Loose, comfortable clothing, closed shoes or sandals you don't mind getting sandy, and a scarf or hat for sun and wind protection are all worth having for the trek.
