Morocco Sahara Desert Tour: 7 Days, Casablanca to Marrakech via Fes

Discover the ultimate Morocco desert road trip on this private 7-day journey from Casablanca to Marrakech. In one unhurried week with Morocco Boutique Tours, you'll explore imperial cities, wander ancient medinas, and spend two nights at an authentic luxury Sahara camp beneath the towering dunes of Merzouga — with your own private driver-guide at your side throughout.

Duration7 Days / 6 Nights
StylePrivate & Chauffeur-Driven
RouteCasablanca → Marrakech
MealsBreakfast Daily + 3 Dinners
Day-by-Day Itinerary

Casablanca to Marrakech in seven private days

Day
1

Casablanca Airport Pick-Up to Imperial Rabat and Fes

A Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide meets you directly at Mohammed V International Airport (CMN) in Casablanca to begin your private Morocco tour. You'll drive to the imperial capital of Rabat to explore the coastal Kasbah of the Oudayas, famous for its blue-and-white alleyways, and admire the historic Hassan Tower. After lunch, continue on to Fes, the spiritual heart of Morocco, and settle into a handpicked luxury riad for the night.

~315 km / 196 mi · approx. 4h15 driveOvernight: Riad Fes Mahal or La Perle de la MedinaBreakfast Included
Day
2

Guided Fes Medina Sightseeing Tour

Meet your Morocco Boutique Tours local guide for an immersive dive into Fes el-Bali, a UNESCO World Heritage site and the largest living medieval medina in the world. Wander the labyrinthine souks, witness the ancient art of leather-making at the Chouara Tanneries, and visit the historic Al-Qarawiyyin University. Enjoy a free evening back at your boutique riad before heading toward the deep desert tomorrow.

Overnight: Riad Fes Mahal or La Perle de la MedinaBreakfast Included
Day
3

Fes to Azrou Barbary Apes and Merzouga Sahara Desert

Depart Fes and travel south through the Middle Atlas Mountains, stopping in the scenic cedar forests of Azrou to see wild Barbary apes in their natural habitat. Your route continues through the Ziz Valley oasis as the landscape shifts into the golden Erg Chebbi dunes. At the gateway to the Sahara, you'll begin a bucket-list camel trek across the rolling sand waves, reaching your luxury desert camp just in time for a traditional dinner and a Berber music show around the campfire under a sky full of stars.

~470 km / 292 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: Tiziri Camp or Sahara Eden CampBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
4

Sahara Desert Excursion and Nomadic Culture

Wake early for a magical sunrise over the high dunes, then breakfast at camp before boarding a 4x4 for an off-road Sahara excursion. Visit a remote settlement to share mint tea with local nomads who preserve centuries-old desert traditions, then head to the village of Khamlia for a spiritual Gnawa music performance. Round out the day with a stop at the seasonal Dayet Srji flamingo lake before returning to camp for a second night of desert stargazing.

~70 km / 43 mi · approx. 3h (off-road 4x4) driveOvernight: Tiziri Camp or Sahara Eden CampBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
5

Merzouga to Todra Gorges and Dades Valley

Leave the dunes behind and drive toward the dramatic Todra Gorges, where 300-meter limestone canyon walls tower overhead — a favorite for rock climbers and nature lovers alike. Continue along the iconic Road of a Thousand Kasbahs into the winding Dades Valley, where you'll unwind over a tranquil dinner tucked into the gorge.

~230 km / 143 mi · approx. 4h30 driveOvernight: Dades Paradise or Dar BluesBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
6

Dades Valley to the High Atlas Mountains and Marrakech

Cross the rugged High Atlas Mountains via the famous Tizi n'Tichka pass, at an altitude of roughly 2,260 meters, with panoramic views over traditional Berber villages and ancient kasbahs. Descend into the vibrant Red City of Marrakech to check into your boutique riad and spend the evening exploring the electric energy of Jemaa el-Fnaa square, with dinner at your leisure.

~330 km / 205 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: Riad Nessma and Spa or Riad Touda BBBreakfast Included
Day
7

Marrakech Sightseeing Tour and Departure

Enjoy a guided Marrakech city tour after breakfast, discovering architectural landmarks such as the Bahia Palace, the Koutoubia Mosque, and the vibrant, colorful artisan souks. Your unforgettable 7-day Casablanca to Marrakech desert tour concludes in the afternoon, leaving you with memories of boutique riads, a luxury Sahara camp, and a seamless private journey with Morocco Boutique Tours.

~6 km / 4 mi · approx. 15 min driveBreakfast Included

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Is this a private tour, or will we share a vehicle with other travelers?

This 7-day Casablanca → Marrakech journey is entirely private — you'll have your own driver-guide and vehicle for your party alone, from pick-up to drop-off, with no other travelers joining your group.

What's included in the €1,157 per-person price?

The €1,157 per person (twin/double share) rate covers your private driver-guide and vehicle, handpicked accommodation each night, daily breakfast and 3 dinners, and all site and monument entrance fees noted in the itinerary. International flights, most lunches, and personal expenses aren't included — see the full breakdown above.

Can this itinerary be adjusted to our dates or interests?

Yes — since it's a private departure, you choose your own start date rather than joining a fixed group. If you'd like the Casablanca → Marrakech route itself reshaped around your interests, our Tailor-Made service can rebuild it from scratch.

What's the best time of year for this route?

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) offer the most comfortable temperatures across Morocco, including the Sahara sections of this itinerary. Summer brings intense desert heat, and desert nights turn cold in winter.

Is this trip physically demanding?

No special fitness is required. The camel trek and any walking sections are paced for everyday travelers, and your driver-guide handles the logistics — you're free to go at your own pace throughout the 7 days.
