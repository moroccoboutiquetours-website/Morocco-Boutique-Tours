Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Morocco Boutique Tours ("we", "us", "our") operates moroccoboutiquetours.com. This page explains what personal information we collect when you visit or use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit our contact/enquiry form, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address, phone number, and details about the trip you're interested in. We use this only to respond to your enquiry and, if you book with us, to plan and deliver your trip. We do not sell or rent this information to third parties.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use analytics tools to understand how visitors use our site — which pages are viewed, how visitors arrived, and general location and device information (not your name or contact details). We currently use Google Analytics for this purpose.

Analytics cookies are only set after you accept them via the cookie banner shown on your first visit. You can decline analytics cookies, or change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain and reloading the page.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar local storage:

  • Necessary: a record of your cookie consent choice, so we don't ask again on every visit.
  • Analytics (optional, off by default): Google Analytics cookies, used only if you accept them, to measure site traffic and improve the site.

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to run this site and process enquiries:

  • Netlify — hosts this website and processes contact form submissions.
  • Google Analytics — website traffic analytics (only with your consent).
  • Google Search Console / Bing Webmaster Tools — used by us to monitor how the site appears in search results; these do not collect personal information about visitors.

Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy policies and may store information outside of Morocco, including in the European Economic Area or the United States.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Data retention

We keep enquiry and booking information for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry, deliver your trip, and meet our legal and accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "last updated" date above will always reflect the latest revision.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at moroccoboutiquetours@gmail.com or +212 771 822 758.
