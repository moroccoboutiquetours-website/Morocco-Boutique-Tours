Morocco Imperial Cities & Sahara Desert Tour: 8 Days from Casablanca

Experience the definitive Morocco desert road trip on this masterfully designed 8-day private itinerary with Morocco Boutique Tours. Journey from the coastal highlights of Casablanca and Rabat to the blue-hued streets of Chefchaouen, through ancient Roman ruins, into the Erg Chebbi dunes of the Merzouga Sahara Desert, and over the Atlas Mountains to Marrakech.

Duration8 Days / 7 Nights
StylePrivate & Chauffeur-Driven
RouteCasablanca → Marrakech (or Casablanca)
MealsBreakfast Daily + 2 Dinners
Day-by-Day Itinerary

Imperial cities, the Blue City, and the Sahara in eight days

Day
1

Casablanca Airport Pick-Up to Imperial Rabat and Chefchaouen

Your Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide meets you at Mohammed V International Airport (CMN) or your Casablanca hotel to begin your private Morocco tour. Drive along the Atlantic coast to Rabat to explore the coastal Kasbah of the Oudayas and the historic Hassan Tower. After lunch, head deep into the Rif Mountains to reach the world-famous blue city of Chefchaouen, where you can stroll the color-washed lanes before settling into your boutique lodging.

~345 km / 214 mi · approx. 5h driveOvernight: Riad Dar Echchaouen or Riad CherifaBreakfast Included
Day
2

Chefchaouen to Volubilis, Imperial Meknes, and Fes

Depart the Blue City after breakfast and travel south toward the Roman ruins of Volubilis, a UNESCO World Heritage site with remarkably preserved mosaics and grand arches. Continue to neighboring Meknes to admire the monumental Bab Mansour gate and historic Lahdim Square, then make the short drive to Fes, the spiritual capital of Morocco, to check into your luxury riad.

~200 km / 124 mi · approx. 3h30 driveOvernight: Riad Fes Mahal or La Perle de la MedinaBreakfast Included
Day
3

Guided Fes Medina Sightseeing Tour

Meet your local expert guide for an immersive dive into Fes el-Bali, the largest living medieval medina in the world. Wander the labyrinthine souks, watch the ancient art of leather-making at the Chouara Tanneries, and visit the Bou Inania Madrasa and Al-Qarawiyyin University. Enjoy a free evening in your boutique riad before your journey to the great desert.

Overnight: Riad Fes Mahal or La Perle de la MedinaBreakfast Included
Day
4

Fes to Azrou Barbary Apes and Merzouga Sahara Desert

Travel south through the Middle Atlas Mountains, stopping in the cedar forests of Azrou to spot wild Barbary apes. Continue through the High Atlas foothills and the dramatic Ziz Valley oasis as the scenery transitions into rolling Erg Chebbi dunes. At Merzouga, gateway to the Sahara, begin your camel trek across the sand waves to reach your luxury desert camp for a traditional dinner and Berber music show around the campfire.

~470 km / 292 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: Tiziri Camp or Sahara Eden CampBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
5

Merzouga Desert Sunrise to Todra Gorges and Dades Valley

Wake for a spectacular sunrise over the high dunes before breakfast at camp. Leave the desert behind and drive toward the jaw-dropping Todra Gorges, where 300-meter limestone walls rise above the canyon floor. Continue along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs into the Dades Valley for a tranquil dinner tucked into the gorge.

~230 km / 143 mi · approx. 4h30 driveOvernight: Dades Paradise or Dar BluesBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
6

Dades Valley to the High Atlas Mountains and Marrakech

Cross the rugged High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka pass, roughly 2,260 meters at its highest point, with panoramic views of Berber villages and ancient kasbahs. Descend into the Red City of Marrakech to check into your boutique riad and spend the evening in the electric energy of Jemaa el-Fnaa square, with dinner at your leisure.

~330 km / 205 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: Riad Nessma and Spa or Riad Touda BBBreakfast Included
Day
7

Marrakech Sightseeing Tour

Enjoy a guided Marrakech city tour after breakfast, taking in the Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs, the Koutoubia Mosque, and the colorful artisan souks. In the afternoon, explore the modern Gueliz district, visit the Majorelle Garden, or relax at your riad's spa before your final night in Morocco.

Overnight: Riad Nessma and Spa or Riad Touda BBBreakfast Included
Day
8

Departure Transfer to Marrakech or Casablanca Airport (CMN)

Savor a final breakfast at the riad before completing your journey. Depending on your flight schedule, your Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide provides a seamless private transfer to either Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) or Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport (CMN).

~6–240 km / 4–149 mi · Marrakech RAK ~15 min, Casablanca CMN ~2h45 driveBreakfast Included

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Is this a private tour, or will we share a vehicle with other travelers?

This 8-day Casablanca → Marrakech (or Casablanca) journey is entirely private — you'll have your own driver-guide and vehicle for your party alone, from pick-up to drop-off, with no other travelers joining your group.

What's included in the €1,332 per-person price?

The €1,332 per person (twin/double share) rate covers your private driver-guide and vehicle, handpicked accommodation each night, daily breakfast and 2 dinners, and all site and monument entrance fees noted in the itinerary. International flights, most lunches, and personal expenses aren't included — see the full breakdown above.

Can this itinerary be adjusted to our dates or interests?

Yes — since it's a private departure, you choose your own start date rather than joining a fixed group. If you'd like the Casablanca → Marrakech (or Casablanca) route itself reshaped around your interests, our Tailor-Made service can rebuild it from scratch.

What's the best time of year for this route?

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) offer the most comfortable temperatures across Morocco, including the Sahara sections of this itinerary. Summer brings intense desert heat, and desert nights turn cold in winter.

Is this trip physically demanding?

No special fitness is required. The camel trek and any walking sections are paced for everyday travelers, and your driver-guide handles the logistics — you're free to go at your own pace throughout the 8 days.
