FAQs

Planning your trip

Answers to the questions we're asked most. Don't see yours? Reach out and we'll respond personally.

Booking & Payments

How do I book a Signature Journey or Tailor-Made trip?

Send an enquiry through our contact form or email us directly. We'll confirm availability and send a proposal with pricing before any payment is required.

What's the deposit and payment schedule?

A 10% deposit secures your booking and accommodation, payable in Euros or Moroccan Dirhams — this amount is deducted from your final balance, and your tour is officially confirmed once the deposit is received. The remaining balance can be settled any time before arrival, or in cash (EUR or MAD) on arrival.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit card payment via a secure payment link (no additional fees), or PayPal (a 5% fee applies to cover processing charges). The remaining balance can also be paid in cash, in EUR or MAD, on arrival.

What is your cancellation policy?

More than 30 days before arrival, your deposit is non-refundable. Between 15 and 30 days before arrival, 75% of the total tour cost is charged. Less than 15 days before arrival, or in the case of a no-show, 100% of the total tour cost is charged. In exceptional circumstances — such as travel restrictions — we'll do our best to offer flexible solutions like rescheduling or credit toward future travel.

Your Trip

Do I share a vehicle or guide with other travelers?

No — every Signature Journey is entirely private. You'll have your own driver-guide and private vehicle for your party alone, from Casablanca airport pickup to final drop-off.

Can a Signature Journey be adjusted to fit my dates?

Yes — because each journey is private, you choose your own start date rather than joining a fixed departure. If you'd like the route itself reshaped around your interests, a Tailor-Made trip can be built from scratch instead.

Is Morocco a good destination for solo travelers?

Yes — with a private driver-guide accompanying you throughout, solo travelers get personal attention and support at every stop without needing to join a group of strangers.

What's Included

What's included in the tour price?

Accommodation, a private guide, ground transport, and select meals and experiences as outlined per itinerary. International flights are not included.

Do you arrange airport transfers?

Yes, airport transfers are included for all Signature Journeys and Tailor-Made trips.

Practical Info

What's the best time of year to visit Morocco?

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) offer the most comfortable temperatures across the country, including the Sahara.

Is Morocco safe to travel to?

Morocco is a popular and generally safe destination for international travelers. As with any trip, we recommend standard travel precautions, which we'll go over ahead of your journey.

Do I need a visa to visit Morocco?

Visa requirements depend on your nationality. We recommend checking with your local Moroccan consulate or embassy before booking flights.
