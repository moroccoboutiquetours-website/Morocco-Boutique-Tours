Morocco Sahara Desert Tour: 3 Days from Marrakech to Merzouga

The essential Marrakech desert escape. In three private, round-trip days with Morocco Boutique Tours, cross the High Atlas Mountains to the kasbah of Ait Benhaddou, wind through the Dades Valley, and spend a night camel trekking and camping beneath the dunes of Merzouga before returning to Marrakech.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

A private round trip from Marrakech to the Sahara in three days

Day
1

Marrakech to the Dades Valley via the High Atlas & Ait Benhaddou

Your Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide collects you from your Marrakech riad or hotel for an early departure into the High Atlas Mountains, crossing the legendary Tizi n'Tichka pass at roughly 2,260 meters for sweeping views over Berber villages terraced into the hillsides. Descending the far side, you reach Ait Benhaddou, the UNESCO-listed fortified kasbah of packed red earth made famous by countless film productions, for a guided walk through its narrow lanes. The route continues through Ouarzazate, Morocco's own "Hollywood," and along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through the rose-scented Skoura and Dades valleys, ending among the folded red cliffs of the Dades Gorge.

~330 km / 205 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: a kasbah-style hotel in the Dades ValleyBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
2

Dades Valley to the Merzouga Sahara Desert via Todra Gorges

Leaving the Dades Valley after breakfast, you stop to walk beneath the sheer 300-meter limestone walls of the Todra Gorges, a dramatic canyon favored by climbers and photographers alike. From there the road runs east toward the golden horizon of the Sahara, arriving at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes in Merzouga in time for a camel trek across the sand waves and a front-row seat for sunset over the dunes. Dinner, a Berber drum circle around the campfire, and a night beneath an extraordinary canopy of stars await at your luxury desert camp.

~230 km / 143 mi · approx. 4h30 driveOvernight: a luxury desert camp beneath the Merzouga dunesBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
3

Merzouga Desert to Marrakech via the High Atlas

Rise early for a final sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes before breakfast at camp. Retrace the route west through the Dades Valley and back over the dramatic Tizi n'Tichka pass, with a stop at a Berber cooperative to see traditional argan oil pressed by hand, before your driver-guide drops you back at your riad or hotel in Marrakech as the day winds down.

~550 km / 342 mi · approx. 9h driveBreakfast Included

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Is this a private tour, or will we share a vehicle with other travelers?

This 3-day Marrakech → Marrakech (Round Trip) journey is entirely private — you'll have your own driver-guide and vehicle for your party alone, from pick-up to drop-off, with no other travelers joining your group.

What's included in the €495 per-person price?

The €495 per person (twin/double share) rate covers your private driver-guide and vehicle, handpicked accommodation each night, daily breakfast and 2 dinners, and all site and monument entrance fees noted in the itinerary. International flights, most lunches, and personal expenses aren't included — see the full breakdown above.

Can this itinerary be adjusted to our dates or interests?

Yes — since it's a private departure, you choose your own start date rather than joining a fixed group. If you'd like the Marrakech → Marrakech (Round Trip) route itself reshaped around your interests, our Tailor-Made service can rebuild it from scratch.

What's the best time of year for this route?

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) offer the most comfortable temperatures across Morocco, including the Sahara sections of this itinerary. Summer brings intense desert heat, and desert nights turn cold in winter.

Is this trip physically demanding?

No special fitness is required. The camel trek and any walking sections are paced for everyday travelers, and your driver-guide handles the logistics — you're free to go at your own pace throughout the 3 days.
