Morocco Grand Imperial Cities & Sahara Desert Tour: 12 Days from Agadir

Our most complete private circuit, round-trip from Agadir. Over twelve unhurried days with Morocco Boutique Tours, take in guided city tours of Marrakech and Fes, the High Atlas Mountains, two nights in the Merzouga Sahara Desert, the Blue City of Chefchaouen, imperial Rabat, Casablanca, and two nights on the Atlantic coast of Essaouira before returning home.

Duration12 Days / 11 Nights
StylePrivate & Chauffeur-Driven
RouteAgadir → Agadir (Round Trip)
MealsBreakfast Daily + 3 Dinners
Day-by-Day Itinerary

The grand twelve-day circuit, round-trip from Agadir

Day
1

Agadir to Marrakech: A Guided Evening in the Medina

Your Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide collects you from your Agadir accommodation for the scenic drive north to Marrakech. After settling into your riad, meet a local guide for an evening walking tour of the Marrakech Medina — wander the labyrinth of souks, admire the Koutoubia Mosque, and take in Jemaa el-Fnaa square as it fills with food stalls, musicians, and storytellers as evening falls.

~250 km / 155 mi · approx. 3h30 driveOvernight: Riad Nessma and Spa or Riad ToudaBreakfast Included
Day
2

Marrakech to the Dades Valley via the High Atlas & Ait Benhaddou

Your Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide collects you from your Marrakech riad for an early departure into the High Atlas Mountains, crossing the legendary Tizi n'Tichka pass at roughly 2,260 meters for sweeping views over Berber villages terraced into the hillsides. Descending the far side, you reach Ait Benhaddou, the UNESCO-listed fortified kasbah of packed red earth made famous by countless film productions, for a guided walk through its narrow lanes. The route continues through Ouarzazate, Morocco's own "Hollywood," and along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through the rose-scented Skoura and Dades valleys, ending among the folded red cliffs of the Dades Gorge.

~330 km / 205 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: Dades Paradise or Dar BluesBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
3

Dades Valley to the Merzouga Sahara Desert via Todra Gorges & Erfoud

After breakfast, walk beneath the sheer 300-meter limestone walls of the Todra Gorges, a dramatic canyon carved by a mountain river. The route continues through Erfoud, Morocco's fossil capital, as the landscape opens into the golden plains of the Sahara. Arriving at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes in Merzouga, you'll swap your vehicle for a camel and trek across the sand waves for sunset, followed by a traditional Moroccan dinner and Berber drum music around the campfire at your luxury desert camp.

~230 km / 143 mi · approx. 4h30 driveOvernight: Tiziri Camp or Sahara Eden CampBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
4

Merzouga Desert Excursion: Nomads, Gnawa Music & the Rissani Souk

A full day devoted to the Sahara. After breakfast at camp, a 4x4 carries you off-road across the Erg Chebbi dunes to meet nomadic Berber families still living in traditional tents, where you're welcomed with a glass of mint tea and a glimpse of desert life. In the village of Khamlia you'll hear the hypnotic rhythms of Gnawa music performed by descendants of West African tradesmen, before an afternoon visit to Rissani's historic souk. Return to camp for a second night of dinner, drumming, and stars.

~90 km / 56 mi · approx. 3h30 (off-road 4x4) driveOvernight: Tiziri Camp or Sahara Eden CampBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
5

Merzouga to Fes via the Ziz Oasis, Midelt & the Cedar Forests of Azrou

After a final sunrise over the dunes and breakfast at camp, you head north through the Ziz Gorges and the palm groves of the Ziz Oasis before climbing into the Middle Atlas. A lunch stop in the apple-orchard town of Midelt breaks up the drive, followed by the ancient cedar forests of Azrou, where wild Barbary macaques often appear roadside. After a brief pause in the alpine town of Ifrane, you make the final descent into Fes, Morocco's spiritual capital, to settle into your riad for the evening.

~470 km / 292 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: Riad Fez Mahal or La Perle de la MedinaBreakfast Included
Day
6

A Local Guide Through the Fes Medina

With a Morocco Boutique Tours local guide, spend the morning navigating Fes el-Bali's labyrinth of more than 9,000 lanes — one of the largest car-free urban spaces on earth. Highlights include the golden brass doors of the Royal Palace exterior, the spice and copper souks, the centuries-old Chouara Tannery, and Al-Qarawiyyin, often cited as the world's oldest continuously operating university. The walk ends with sweeping medina views from the Merenid Tombs before a free afternoon back at your riad.

Overnight: Riad Fez Mahal or La Perle de la MedinaBreakfast Included
Day
7

Fes to Chefchaouen via Imperial Meknes & Roman Volubilis

Depart Fes for the imperial city of Meknes to see the monumental Bab Mansour gate and the Mausoleum of Moulay Ismail, then continue to the UNESCO-listed Roman ruins of Volubilis for a guided look at remarkably preserved mosaic floors dating back nearly two millennia. From there the road winds into the Rif Mountains and the blue-washed lanes of Chefchaouen, where you'll have the late afternoon free to wander the medina and browse local handicrafts before dinner at your leisure.

~200 km / 124 mi · approx. 3h30 driveOvernight: Dar Echchaouen or Riad CherifaBreakfast Included
Day
8

Chefchaouen to Casablanca via Imperial Rabat

Breakfast overlooking the blue city precedes the drive south to Rabat, Morocco's coastal capital, where you'll see the Mechouar of the Royal Palace, the Hassan Tower beside the Mausoleum of Mohammed V, and the whitewashed Kasbah of the Oudayas above the Atlantic. After lunch, continue down the coast into Casablanca to settle in for the evening.

~340 km / 211 mi · approx. 5h driveOvernight: Val d'Anfa or Casa DiamondBreakfast Included
Day
9

Casablanca to the Atlantic Coast of Essaouira

Begin with a visit to the Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest mosques in the world, built directly over the Atlantic. From Casablanca, the road runs southwest along the coast to Essaouira, a laid-back, fortified port town known for strong sea breezes, whitewashed ramparts, and a thriving arts scene, where you'll settle into your riad in the medina for the evening.

~360 km / 224 mi · approx. 4h30 driveOvernight: Riad Mimouna or Riad MalaikaBreakfast Included
Day
10

Free Day in Essaouira

A day entirely at your own pace. Walk the historic Skala de la Ville ramparts where old cannons still face the sea, browse the fishing port to watch the day's catch come in, or wander the car-free medina for thuya woodwork and handmade silver jewelry. Settle in at a beachside café, sample fresh grilled seafood by the water, or simply watch the sun set over the Mogador islands.

Overnight: Riad Mimouna or Riad MalaikaBreakfast Included
Day
11

Essaouira to Agadir: An Afternoon at Leisure

After breakfast, the coastal road carries you south back to Agadir. The afternoon is yours to unwind — relax on Agadir's ten-kilometer golden sand beach, browse the stalls of Souk El Had, or climb to the Agadir Oufella Kasbah ruins for sweeping views over the bay and the city below.

~175 km / 109 mi · approx. 2h30 driveOvernight: your hotel or riad in AgadirBreakfast Included
Day
12

Free Morning in Agadir & Departure

Enjoy a relaxed final morning in Agadir at your own pace — stroll the ten-kilometer golden sand beach, browse the stalls of Souk El Had, or climb to the Agadir Oufella Kasbah ruins for panoramic views over the bay — before your Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide provides a private transfer to Agadir Al Massira Airport (AGA) or your accommodation.

~22 km / 14 mi · approx. 30 min driveBreakfast Included

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Is this a private tour, or will we share a vehicle with other travelers?

This 12-day Agadir → Agadir (Round Trip) journey is entirely private — you'll have your own driver-guide and vehicle for your party alone, from pick-up to drop-off, with no other travelers joining your group.

What's included in the €2,099 per-person price?

The €2,099 per person (twin/double share) rate covers your private driver-guide and vehicle, handpicked accommodation each night, daily breakfast and 3 dinners, and all site and monument entrance fees noted in the itinerary. International flights, most lunches, and personal expenses aren't included — see the full breakdown above.

Can this itinerary be adjusted to our dates or interests?

Yes — since it's a private departure, you choose your own start date rather than joining a fixed group. If you'd like the Agadir → Agadir (Round Trip) route itself reshaped around your interests, our Tailor-Made service can rebuild it from scratch.

What's the best time of year for this route?

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) offer the most comfortable temperatures across Morocco, including the Sahara sections of this itinerary. Summer brings intense desert heat, and desert nights turn cold in winter.

Is this trip physically demanding?

No special fitness is required. The camel trek and any walking sections are paced for everyday travelers, and your driver-guide handles the logistics — you're free to go at your own pace throughout the 12 days.
