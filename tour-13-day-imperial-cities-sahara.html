Morocco Imperial Cities & Sahara Desert Tour: 13-Day Grand Loop from Casablanca

Experience the ultimate, comprehensive Morocco desert road trip on this masterfully designed 13-day private itinerary from Morocco Boutique Tours. Your extensive loop begins and ends in Casablanca, taking in the coastal history of Rabat, the blue-hued streets of Chefchaouen, ancient Roman ruins, the spiritual heart of Fes, the Erg Chebbi dunes of the Sahara, dramatic gorges, the Atlas Mountains, Marrakech, and two full nights on the Atlantic coast of Essaouira.

Duration13 Days / 12 Nights
StylePrivate & Chauffeur-Driven
RouteCasablanca → Casablanca (loop)
MealsBreakfast Daily + 3 Dinners
Day-by-Day Itinerary

A complete private loop from Casablanca and back

Day
1

Casablanca Airport Pick-Up to Imperial Rabat

Your Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide meets you directly at Mohammed V International Airport (CMN) or your Casablanca hotel to begin your private Morocco tour. Drive along the Atlantic coast to Rabat to explore the coastal Kasbah of the Oudayas and the historic Hassan Tower before checking into your boutique riad for a relaxing evening.

~115 km / 71 mi · approx. 1h15 driveOvernight: Ryad Zyo or Riad KalaaBreakfast Included
Day
2

Rabat to the Blue City of Chefchaouen

Head deep into the scenic Rif Mountains to reach the world-famous blue city of Chefchaouen. Spend the afternoon wandering the magical, color-washed lanes and relaxing at a local café in Outa el-Hammam square before settling into your boutique lodging.

~230 km / 143 mi · approx. 3h30 driveOvernight: Riad Dar Echchaouen or Riad CherifaBreakfast Included
Day
3

Chefchaouen to Volubilis, Imperial Meknes, and Fes

Travel south toward the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis, a UNESCO World Heritage site, then continue to Meknes to marvel at the monumental Bab Mansour gate and historic Lahdim Square. In the late afternoon, drive to Fes to check into your luxury riad.

~200 km / 124 mi · approx. 3h30 driveOvernight: Riad Fes Mahal or La Perle de la MedinaBreakfast Included
Day
4

Guided Fes Medina Sightseeing Tour

Meet your Morocco Boutique Tours local guide for an immersive dive into Fes el-Bali, the largest living medieval medina in the world. Wander the labyrinthine souks, witness leather-making at the Chouara Tanneries, and visit the Bou Inania Madrasa and Al-Qarawiyyin University before a free evening in the cultural heart of Morocco.

Overnight: Riad Fes Mahal or La Perle de la MedinaBreakfast Included
Day
5

Fes to Azrou Barbary Apes and Merzouga Sahara Desert

Travel south through the Middle Atlas Mountains, stopping in the cedar forests of Azrou for wild Barbary apes, then continue through the Ziz Valley oasis as the landscape transitions into the golden Erg Chebbi dunes. Begin your camel trek across the sand waves to reach your luxury desert camp for a traditional dinner and Berber music show.

~470 km / 292 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: Tiziri Camp or Sahara Eden CampBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
6

Sahara Desert Excursion and Nomadic Culture

Wake early for a sunrise over the high dunes, then board a 4x4 for an off-road Sahara excursion to visit a remote settlement and share mint tea with local nomads. Visit the village of Khamlia for a Gnawa music performance and stop at the seasonal Dayet Srji flamingo lake before a second night of desert stargazing.

~70 km / 43 mi · approx. 3h (off-road 4x4) driveOvernight: Tiziri Camp or Sahara Eden CampBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
7

Merzouga Desert Sunrise to Todra Gorges and Dades Valley

Catch a final desert sunrise before breakfast at camp, then drive toward the jaw-dropping Todra Gorges and its 300-meter limestone walls. Continue along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs into the Dades Valley for a tranquil dinner tucked into the gorge.

~230 km / 143 mi · approx. 4h30 driveOvernight: Dades Paradise or Dar BluesBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
8

Dades Valley to the High Atlas Mountains and Marrakech

Cross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka pass, roughly 2,260 meters at its highest point, with panoramic views of Berber villages and ancient kasbahs. Descend into the Red City of Marrakech and spend the evening in the electric energy of Jemaa el-Fnaa square.

~330 km / 205 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: Riad Nessma and Spa or Riad Touda BBBreakfast Included
Day
9

Marrakech Sightseeing Tour

Enjoy a guided Marrakech city tour, discovering the Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs, the Koutoubia Mosque, and the colorful artisan souks. Spend the afternoon exploring Gueliz, visiting the Majorelle Garden, or relaxing at your riad's spa.

Overnight: Riad Nessma and Spa or Riad Touda BBBreakfast Included
Day
10

Marrakech to the Atlantic Coast of Essaouira

Drive west to the charming, fortified beach town of Essaouira. Explore the white-and-blue medina, walk the historic sea-facing ramparts, and savor freshly caught seafood at the bustling port before your first evening by the sea.

~190 km / 118 mi · approx. 2h45 driveOvernight: Riad Mimouna or Riad MalaikaBreakfast Included
Day
11

Free Day Exploring Essaouira

Enjoy a completely free day to experience Essaouira's coastal charm — stroll the relaxed medina souks, browse local art galleries, try windsurfing on the expansive beach, or take in the Atlantic views from the Skala de la Ville ramparts. Spend a second restful night in your oceanfront haven.

Overnight: Riad Mimouna or Riad MalaikaBreakfast Included
Day
12

Essaouira to Casablanca via the Coastal Route

Set off on a scenic drive up the Atlantic coast back toward Casablanca, stopping at panoramic viewpoints, coastal villages, or historic Portuguese fortifications in Safi or El Jadida. Arrive in Morocco's economic capital in the afternoon for a final evening stroll past the grand Hassan II Mosque or along the Ain Diab Corniche.

~350 km / 217 mi · approx. 4h30 driveOvernight: Hotel Club Val d'Anfa or Casa DiamondBreakfast Included
Day
13

Departure Transfer to Casablanca Airport (CMN)

Enjoy a final breakfast before your Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide provides a private, seamless transfer to Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport (CMN), completing your 13-day grand loop of Morocco.

~30 km / 19 mi · approx. 40 min driveBreakfast Included

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Is this a private tour, or will we share a vehicle with other travelers?

This 13-day Casablanca → Casablanca (loop) journey is entirely private — you'll have your own driver-guide and vehicle for your party alone, from pick-up to drop-off, with no other travelers joining your group.

What's included in the €2,080 per-person price?

The €2,080 per person (twin/double share) rate covers your private driver-guide and vehicle, handpicked accommodation each night, daily breakfast and 3 dinners, and all site and monument entrance fees noted in the itinerary. International flights, most lunches, and personal expenses aren't included — see the full breakdown above.

Can this itinerary be adjusted to our dates or interests?

Yes — since it's a private departure, you choose your own start date rather than joining a fixed group. If you'd like the Casablanca → Casablanca (loop) route itself reshaped around your interests, our Tailor-Made service can rebuild it from scratch.

What's the best time of year for this route?

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) offer the most comfortable temperatures across Morocco, including the Sahara sections of this itinerary. Summer brings intense desert heat, and desert nights turn cold in winter.

Is this trip physically demanding?

No special fitness is required. The camel trek and any walking sections are paced for everyday travelers, and your driver-guide handles the logistics — you're free to go at your own pace throughout the 13 days.
