Morocco Sahara Desert Tour: 3 Days from Agadir to Merzouga

The essential Agadir desert escape. In three private, round-trip days with Morocco Boutique Tours, cross the High Atlas Mountains to the kasbah of Ait Benhaddou, wind through the Dades and Todra gorges, and spend a night camel trekking and camping beneath the dunes of Merzouga before returning to the Atlantic coast.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

A private round trip from Agadir to the Sahara in three days

Day
1

Agadir to the Dades Valley via the High Atlas & Ait Benhaddou

Your Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide collects you from your Agadir accommodation for an early departure into the High Atlas Mountains. The route climbs through changing landscapes to Ait Benhaddou, the UNESCO-listed fortified kasbah of packed red earth made famous by countless film productions, for a guided walk through its ancient lanes. Continuing through Ouarzazate, Morocco's own "Hollywood," and along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through the rose-scented Skoura and Dades valleys, you'll arrive among the folded red cliffs of the Dades Gorge by evening.

~420 km / 261 mi · approx. 7h driveOvernight: Dades Paradise or Dar BluesBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
2

Dades Valley to the Merzouga Sahara Desert via Todra Gorges & Erfoud

After breakfast, walk beneath the sheer 300-meter limestone walls of the Todra Gorges, a dramatic canyon carved by a mountain river. The route continues through Erfoud, Morocco's fossil capital, as the landscape opens into the golden plains of the Sahara. Arriving at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes in Merzouga, you'll swap your vehicle for a camel and trek across the sand waves for sunset, followed by a traditional Moroccan dinner and Berber drum music around the campfire at your luxury desert camp.

~230 km / 143 mi · approx. 4h30 driveOvernight: Tiziri Camp or Sahara Eden CampBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
3

Merzouga Desert to Agadir via Rissani & Ouarzazate

Rise early for a final sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes before breakfast at camp. The return journey passes through Rissani, the historic market town and birthplace of Morocco's ruling Alaouite dynasty, before crossing back through the Anti-Atlas via Ouarzazate, where you'll pause for lunch. Your driver-guide continues the drive down to the Atlantic coast, delivering you to your Agadir hotel or riad by evening.

~705 km / 438 mi · approx. 10h driveBreakfast Included

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Is this a private tour, or will we share a vehicle with other travelers?

This 3-day Agadir → Agadir (Round Trip) journey is entirely private — you'll have your own driver-guide and vehicle for your party alone, from pick-up to drop-off, with no other travelers joining your group.

What's included in the €554 per-person price?

The €554 per person (twin/double share) rate covers your private driver-guide and vehicle, handpicked accommodation each night, daily breakfast and 2 dinners, and all site and monument entrance fees noted in the itinerary. International flights, most lunches, and personal expenses aren't included — see the full breakdown above.

Can this itinerary be adjusted to our dates or interests?

Yes — since it's a private departure, you choose your own start date rather than joining a fixed group. If you'd like the Agadir → Agadir (Round Trip) route itself reshaped around your interests, our Tailor-Made service can rebuild it from scratch.

What's the best time of year for this route?

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) offer the most comfortable temperatures across Morocco, including the Sahara sections of this itinerary. Summer brings intense desert heat, and desert nights turn cold in winter.

Is this trip physically demanding?

No special fitness is required. The camel trek and any walking sections are paced for everyday travelers, and your driver-guide handles the logistics — you're free to go at your own pace throughout the 3 days.
