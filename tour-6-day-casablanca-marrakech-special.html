Morocco Special Tour: 6 Days from Casablanca to Marrakech

A fast-paced private introduction to Morocco, from Casablanca to Marrakech in six unhurried days. With Morocco Boutique Tours, you'll trace the Rif Mountains to the blue lanes of Chefchaouen, spend a full day exploring Fes with a local guide, ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes for a night in the Sahara, and cross the High Atlas via Ait Benhaddou before arriving in Marrakech.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Casablanca to Marrakech in six private days

Day
1

Casablanca Airport Pick-Up to Rabat & the Blue City of Chefchaouen

A Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide welcomes you at Mohammed V International Airport (CMN) or your Casablanca hotel to begin your private tour. Head first to Rabat, Morocco's coastal capital, to see the Kasbah of the Oudayas, the unfinished Hassan Tower, and the Royal Palace grounds from outside. From there, the road climbs through wheat fields and the folded hills of the Rif Mountains before dropping into Chefchaouen, where every wall and stairwell is washed in a different shade of blue.

~345 km / 214 mi · approx. 5h driveOvernight: Riad Dar Echchaouen or Riad CherifaBreakfast Included
Day
2

Chefchaouen to Volubilis, Meknes & Fes

Spend the morning wandering Chefchaouen's cool blue lanes before setting off for the Roman ruins of Volubilis, where mosaic floors and toppled columns still trace the outline of a 3rd-century city. Continue to Meknes to pass through the monumental Bab Mansour gate and the open expanse of Place El Hedim, then finish the day in Fes, where a handpicked riad waits inside the old medina walls.

~200 km / 124 mi · approx. 3h30 driveOvernight: Riad Fes Mahal or La Perle de la MedinaBreakfast Included
Day
3

A Local Guide Through the Fes Medina

Fes el-Bali unfolds slowly, and a full day is barely enough to take it in. With a Morocco Boutique Tours local guide, pass through the Blue Gate into the world's largest car-free urban quarter, then visit the Bou Inania Madrasa, glimpse the Qarawiyyin Mosque, and thread through souks that have barely changed their layout in a thousand years. The walk ends at the Chouara Tanneries, where hides are still dyed by hand in stone vats much as they were centuries ago.

Overnight: Riad Fes Mahal or La Perle de la MedinaBreakfast Included
Day
4

Fes to Ifrane, Azrou & the Merzouga Sahara Desert

Leave Fes behind for Ifrane, a mountain town of steep gabled roofs sometimes called Morocco's Switzerland, then continue to the cedar forests of Azrou, home to a shy population of wild Barbary apes. The route presses on through Midelt and the palm-lined Ziz Valley as the scenery turns from mountain to desert. By late afternoon you're mounting a camel at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes for the ride into camp, where dinner and a Berber drum circle await under a sky thick with stars.

~470 km / 292 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: Tiziri Camp or Sahara Eden CampBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
5

Merzouga Sunrise to Todra Gorges & the Dades Valley

Watch the dunes catch the first light before breakfast at camp, then head into Rissani to browse a souk that feels a world away from Marrakech's. From there the road runs to the Todra Gorges, where a river has carved a passage through 300 meters of sheer rock, before continuing along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs into the Dades Valley, its cliffs folded like pastry above fields of fig and walnut trees.

~230 km / 143 mi · approx. 4h30 driveOvernight: Dades Paradise or Dar BluesBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
6

Dades Valley to the Valley of Roses, Ait Benhaddou & Marrakech

Breakfast in the Dades Valley is followed by a drive through the Valley of Roses and the palm groves of Skoura, where the centuries-old Amridil Kasbah still stands. In Ouarzazate, see the Taourirt Kasbah before reaching Ait Benhaddou, the UNESCO-listed mudbrick village familiar from films like Gladiator and Game of Thrones. The final climb over the High Atlas via the Tizi n'Tichka pass, roughly 2,260 meters at its peak, brings you down into Marrakech, where your private 6-day journey with Morocco Boutique Tours comes to a close.

~330 km / 205 mi · approx. 6h30 driveBreakfast Included

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Is this a private tour, or will we share a vehicle with other travelers?

This 6-day Casablanca → Marrakech journey is entirely private — you'll have your own driver-guide and vehicle for your party alone, from pick-up to drop-off, with no other travelers joining your group.

What's included in the €988 per-person price?

The €988 per person (twin/double share) rate covers your private driver-guide and vehicle, handpicked accommodation each night, daily breakfast and 2 dinners, and all site and monument entrance fees noted in the itinerary. International flights, most lunches, and personal expenses aren't included — see the full breakdown above.

Can this itinerary be adjusted to our dates or interests?

Yes — since it's a private departure, you choose your own start date rather than joining a fixed group. If you'd like the Casablanca → Marrakech route itself reshaped around your interests, our Tailor-Made service can rebuild it from scratch.

What's the best time of year for this route?

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) offer the most comfortable temperatures across Morocco, including the Sahara sections of this itinerary. Summer brings intense desert heat, and desert nights turn cold in winter.

Is this trip physically demanding?

No special fitness is required. The camel trek and any walking sections are paced for everyday travelers, and your driver-guide handles the logistics — you're free to go at your own pace throughout the 6 days.
