Destinations

Where we take you

Every itinerary is built from this same set of places — each visited slowly, with time to actually experience it rather than just pass through.

Marrakech

The souks, palaces, and gardens of the Red City — our most common starting point, and worth two or three unhurried days on its own.

Sahara Desert (Merzouga)

Towering dunes, a private luxury camp, and a night sky far from any city light — the highlight of most itineraries.

Atlas Mountains

Berber villages, mountain passes, and valley trails within easy reach of Marrakech — ideal for a day or an overnight.

Fes

Morocco's spiritual and cultural capital, with the largest car-free urban area in the world inside its ancient medina walls.

Chefchaouen

The Blue City, tucked into the Rif Mountains — quieter, cooler, and one of the most photographed towns in the country.

Essaouira

A breezy Atlantic port town with whitewashed ramparts, fresh seafood, and a noticeably slower pace.

Ouarzazate

Gateway to the Sahara, known for its kasbahs and film studios — a scenic stop along the Kasbah Route.

Rabat

Morocco's coastal capital — a calmer, more understated counterpoint to Marrakech and Fes, easily added to longer itineraries.

Want to combine a few of these?

Every Signature Journey blends several destinations — or we'll build a route around the ones you choose.
