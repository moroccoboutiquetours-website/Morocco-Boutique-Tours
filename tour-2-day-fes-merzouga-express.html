Morocco Sahara Desert Express Tour: 2 Days from Fes to Merzouga

The fastest way to reach the Sahara from Fes. This private, round-trip 2-day desert express tour crosses the Middle Atlas via Ifrane and Azrou to a night camel trekking and camping beneath the Merzouga dunes, with a direct return to Fes the next day.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

A private round trip from Fes to the Sahara in two days

Day
1

Fes to the Merzouga Sahara Desert via Ifrane & Azrou

Your Morocco Boutique Tours driver-guide collects you in Fes for the drive south into the Middle Atlas, stopping first in Ifrane, often nicknamed Morocco's Switzerland for its alpine architecture and clean mountain air. Continuing through the cedar forests of Azrou, keep watch for wild Barbary macaques often seen roadside, before the landscape opens into the dramatic red canyons and palm groves of the Ziz Valley oasis. By late afternoon you'll reach the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes in Merzouga for a camel trek across the sand and a front-row seat for sunset over the Sahara, followed by dinner and Berber drum music around the campfire at your luxury desert camp.

~470 km / 292 mi · approx. 6h30 driveOvernight: Tiziri Camp or Sahara Eden CampBreakfast & Dinner Included
Day
2

Merzouga Sahara Desert to Fes via Ifrane & Azrou

Catch a final sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes before breakfast at camp, then retrace the route north through the Ziz Valley and the Middle Atlas. Stop once more in the cedar forests of Azrou, keeping an eye out for Barbary macaques, before the final descent through Ifrane back into Fes, where your driver-guide provides a private drop-off at your riad or hotel.

~470 km / 292 mi · approx. 6h30 driveBreakfast Included

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Is this a private tour, or will we share a vehicle with other travelers?

This 2-day Fes → Fes (Round Trip) journey is entirely private — you'll have your own driver-guide and vehicle for your party alone, from pick-up to drop-off, with no other travelers joining your group.

What's included in the €425 per-person price?

The €425 per person (twin/double share) rate covers your private driver-guide and vehicle, handpicked accommodation each night, daily breakfast and 1 dinner, and all site and monument entrance fees noted in the itinerary. International flights, most lunches, and personal expenses aren't included — see the full breakdown above.

Can this itinerary be adjusted to our dates or interests?

Yes — since it's a private departure, you choose your own start date rather than joining a fixed group. If you'd like the Fes → Fes (Round Trip) route itself reshaped around your interests, our Tailor-Made service can rebuild it from scratch.

What's the best time of year for this route?

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) offer the most comfortable temperatures across Morocco, including the Sahara sections of this itinerary. Summer brings intense desert heat, and desert nights turn cold in winter.

Is this trip physically demanding?

No special fitness is required. The camel trek and any walking sections are paced for everyday travelers, and your driver-guide handles the logistics — you're free to go at your own pace throughout the 2 days.
