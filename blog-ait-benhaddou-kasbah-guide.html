Ait Benhaddou: Visiting Morocco's Most Famous Kasbah

Rising from the red earth on the road between Marrakech and the Sahara, Ait Benhaddou is Morocco's most photographed kasbah — and, for many travelers, one of the highlights of the entire trip.

Destinations · 6 min read · Morocco Boutique Tours

What Is Ait Benhaddou?

Ait Benhaddou is a ksar — a fortified village of densely packed earthen buildings — built from packed red clay and straw along the historic caravan route between the Sahara and Marrakech. Its dramatic silhouette and UNESCO World Heritage status make it one of the best-preserved examples of traditional pre-Saharan earthen architecture anywhere in Morocco, and a regular stop on any route between Marrakech and the desert.

A Famous Film Location

Ait Benhaddou's striking architecture has made it one of the most filmed locations in Morocco, standing in for ancient settings in productions including Gladiator, Game of Thrones, Lawrence of Arabia, and Kingdom of Heaven. Ouarzazate, the nearby town nicknamed "Morocco's Hollywood," hosts several major film studios, and it's common to see production crews still working in and around the kasbah today.

What You'll See

A visit typically involves crossing the (usually shallow) Ounila River, then wandering up through the ksar's narrow lanes past shops, a small museum, and the remains of a communal granary at the hilltop, which offers sweeping views back down over the kasbah and surrounding valley. Most of the ksar's original residents have relocated to a more modern village across the river, though a handful of families still live within the old walls.

The Best Time of Day to Visit

Early morning and late afternoon light brings out the deep red tones of the earthen walls far better than the flat light of midday, and most private itineraries time the visit accordingly. If you're traveling with a driver-guide, ask about timing your stop for golden hour if photography is a priority.

Frequently Asked

Visiting Ait Benhaddou: Quick Answers

Is Ait Benhaddou worth visiting?

Yes — its UNESCO-listed earthen architecture, dramatic setting, and film history make it one of the most rewarding stops on any Marrakech-to-Sahara route, and most itineraries include it as standard.

What movies were filmed at Ait Benhaddou?

Ait Benhaddou has featured in numerous major productions, including Gladiator, Lawrence of Arabia, Kingdom of Heaven, and several seasons of Game of Thrones, thanks to its striking, ancient-looking architecture.

How long should you spend at Ait Benhaddou?

Most private tours allow one to two hours, enough time to walk up through the ksar to the hilltop granary and back down, with time to browse the small shops along the way.

What is the difference between Ait Benhaddou and Ouarzazate?

Ait Benhaddou is the historic fortified kasbah itself; Ouarzazate is the larger nearby town, home to major film studios and often used as an overnight stop on longer itineraries.
